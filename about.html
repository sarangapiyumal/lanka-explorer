About Us

We are a small group of web development students who built Lanka Explorer to make planning a trip around Sri Lanka simple. The site brings together destination facts, live weather, entry fees in rupees and an interactive map in one place. We built it with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript to learn how real web apps work.
